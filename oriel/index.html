§ 01The idea

The idea

The Greeks had two words for time. Chronos, the time that passes, and kairos, the moment that matters. We spend eleven months building one watch so it can keep the first, and mark the second.

Every O-1 is assembled, regulated and cased by a single watchmaker, who engraves their initials on the movement before it leaves the bench.

§ 02Anatomy

Seven layers between you and the second.

  1. 01

    Sapphire crystal

    Domed and anti-reflective on both sides. Nine on the Mohs scale.

  2. 02

    Bezel

    Polished, with a 0.3 mm step that catches a single line of light.

  3. 03

    Hands

    Dauphine, faceted by hand, filled with luminescent paint.

  4. 04

    Dial

    Brass, fourteen coats of lacquer, twelve applied indices.

  5. 05

    Movement

    Calibre O-1. 212 components, 31 jewels, regulated in five positions.

  6. 06

    Case

    40 mm across, 11.2 mm thick. Satin flanks, polished bevels.

  7. 07

    Caseback

    Screwed down around a sapphire window. Water resistant to 100 m.

§ 03The movement

Inside, a small universe keeps time.

i / iii

The escapement

Twenty-eight thousand eight hundred times an hour, the pallet fork lets one tooth of the escape wheel go. You hear it as a tick. We hear a heartbeat.

28,800beats per hour

ii / iii

The mainspring

A coiled blade inside the barrel stores the energy of your wrist. Fully wound, it runs for three days, long enough to leave on the nightstand for a weekend.

72hours of reserve

iii / iii

The rotor

A half-moon of solid gold swings with every gesture and winds the spring in both directions. Movement, turned into time.

360°bidirectional winding
