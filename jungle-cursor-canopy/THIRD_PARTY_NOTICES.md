# Third-party assets

`public/assets/leaf-surface-color.webp` and
`public/assets/leaf-surface-normal.webp` are processed derivatives of
“Translucent leaf in macro (Unsplash)”, sourced from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Translucent_leaf_in_macro_(Unsplash).jpg).

The source image is available under the
[CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The derivatives were mirrored into a seamless tile, colour-reduced, contrast-shaped,
and used to generate a tangent-space normal map.

JavaScript dependencies and their versions are listed in `package.json`.
